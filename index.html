On the hook

How it goes on.

01Deck downThe bed tilts and slides back until it meets the road. No ramps, no lifting on a tow bar.
02Winched onCable out, vehicle pulled up on its own wheels. Nothing is dragged and the drivetrain stays out of it.
03Strapped and goneDeck levels, wheels strapped at four points. All four tyres off the road for the whole trip.

Pricing

How a tow gets priced.

Nobody likes a number they didn't expect. Here is what actually goes into the bill, so you can ask for the total before anything is hooked up.

01
Hook-upA flat charge to send a truck and get your vehicle safely on it. Charged once.
02
MileagePer loaded mile from pickup to wherever it's going. Shorter drop, smaller number.
03
Recovery timeOnly when the vehicle isn't sitting on pavement — ditches, mud, rollovers and winching are charged by time on scene.
04
After hoursNights, weekends and holidays carry a different rate than a weekday call.
05
StoragePer day, only if the vehicle sits on the lot. Collect it sooner and this never starts.
06
Card processingCards carry a 4% processing fee. Cash avoids it.
!
Police-ordered tows are differentIf your vehicle was towed at the request of police, the rate is set by the city or the agency's rotation, not by us. We charge what that schedule allows.
!
Insurance and motor clubsMany policies and club memberships cover some or all of a tow. Tell us who you're with when you call and we'll say what's usually covered.
!
Roadside beats a towA jump, a lockout or a tire change is almost always cheaper than hauling the vehicle. Describe the problem and we'll say which one you need.

Ask for the number before we hook up. Tell us where you are and where it's going and you'll get the total on the phone, not on the invoice.
